How the open model works · privacy · credits

Google’s YAMNet (Apache 2.0), running through TensorFlow.js, recognises 521 audio-event labels. We average model scores over short audio frames. Scores are not calibrated probabilities; sound groups and uncertainty cutoffs are simple, editable heuristics.

All inference happens in your browser. This app has no analytics, location access or audio upload endpoint. Loading the site downloads static files from its host. Recordings are held in memory briefly and discarded after analysis. Exported journals contain labels and your written notes, never audio.

After successful preparation, the app and model are cached for offline use. Browser storage can be evicted; check the readiness message before heading out.
